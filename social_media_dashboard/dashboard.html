Intelligence

Engagement Drivers

What makes people like, comment, save, share, and follow. Based on pattern analysis across 221 posts.

What drives likes (directly observed)

  • Community identity content: team reveals, "this is us", association pride (2.17% avg ER)
  • Reels format (2.22% ER) vs Images (1.05%)
  • Posts featuring recognizable faces (speakers, team members, founders)
  • Monday posts accumulate more likes than other days
  • Emotional hooks: pride, belonging, aspiration present in top 10 posts

What drives comments (directly observed)

  • Posts with direct questions show slightly higher engagement (1.87% vs 1.69%), though the difference is small across 57 posts
  • Provocative or contrarian takes (myth-buster hooks in top 10)
  • Interactive formats: True/False games, fill-in-the-blank
  • Reels avg 5.9 comments per post vs 1.8 for images (3.3x ratio)
  • Behind-the-scenes content that sparks curiosity

What likely drives saves (estimated)

  • Educational carousels with frameworks, tips, checklists
  • Longer carousels (7+ slides) with dense, reusable information
  • VC and startup knowledge breakdowns
  • Step-by-step guides and "how to" content
  • Content with genuine rewatch or re-read value
Save counts are not available via public Instagram API. These are estimated from content type, structure, and engagement patterns.

What likely drives shares (estimated)

  • Content that makes the sharer look informed
  • Relatable student or entrepreneur moments
  • Career and internship opportunities (people tag friends)
  • Event announcements friends would want to attend
  • Contrarian takes that spark discussion
Share counts are not available via public Instagram API. These are estimated from comment patterns and content characteristics.

What drives profile visits and follows (estimated)

  • Top reel reached 57,911 views vs 3,890 average, suggesting Explore page distribution. Replicating this pattern creates discovery.
  • The highest-ER post (13.18%) was a collaboration with another account. Cross-posts dramatically amplify reach.
  • Content that triggers "who is this association?" curiosity combined with a clear, specific bio
  • Strategically chosen pinned posts that showcase best work

Action Plan

Prioritized actions based on the data analysis.

If I were the social media manager

The data clearly shows this account performs best when it feels human and educational, not when it pushes announcements. The top 10 posts are dominated by community content, team reveals, and expert insights. The bottom 10 are almost entirely generic event flyers.

Three shifts the data supports:

1. Value before announcement. Every post needs to pass "why should I care?" before publishing. Lead with insight, not logistics. The data shows Long (60+) captions avg 2.22% vs Short (<20 words) at 1.53%.

2. People in every post. Posts with faces and real names cluster heavily in the top quartile. No more faceless graphics.

3. Systematic rhythm. Monday reels, Wednesday carousels, Friday community posts. The data shows Monday at 2.13% ER is strongest, and Reels at 2.22% are the best format.

Single highest-impact action: a weekly "60-Second Lesson" reel combining the best-performing format (Reels) with the best-performing pillar (Community & Culture).
